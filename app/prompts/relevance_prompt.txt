Your task is to determine whether a search result contains useful investor discovery and intelligence, such as:
- Venture capital firm websites, portfolio pages, or team directories
- Curated lists of investors, startup ecosystem databases, or accelerator networks
- Angel investment platforms, venture studios, and funding resources

----------------------------------------
CRITICAL RULES
----------------------------------------
- Base your scoring and classification strictly on the provided Title, URL, and Snippet context. Do NOT assume or hallucinate features of the website not supported by the context.

- Avoid over-filtering: if the page is potentially a startup investor or directory, rate it relevant.

----------------------------------------
SOURCE TYPE CLASSIFICATION
----------------------------------------
Classify the source type as ONE of:
- "investor_profile": The page is a VC firm or investment fund's own website (e.g. team pages, portfolios).
- "investor_mention": A blog post, news article, or listicle mentioning investors.
- "investor_directory": A structured aggregator or database listing multiple investors (e.g. crunchbase, dealroom).
- "unknown": None of the above.

----------------------------------------
SCORING GUIDELINES
----------------------------------------
