# SaaS is Growing Deeper But Now With More Rationality

![](<Base64-Image-Removed>)

India's SaaS Ecosystem is Now Growing Deeper With More Rationality.

SaaS Ecosystem of India is Rapidly Maturing. With Over $20B in Investment and the Rise of AI, Investors are Now Prioritizing Sustainable Business Models and Operational Efficiency, Shifting Away from Sky-High Valuations. As the Industry Eyes a $70B Market Opportunity by 2030, Startups are Innovating and Adapting to Stand Out in an Increasingly Competitive Landscape.

Pune \| November 27, 2024

With the 70 Bn+ Market Opportunity by 2030, India’s SaaS startup ecosystem is no longer in its infancy—it’s rapidly maturing into a dominant force within the country's broader digital economy. From Bengaluru’s bustling tech corridors to the evolving landscapes of Pune and Chennai, SaaS startups are steadily planting their roots across the country, shaping India’s digital economy but now with more realism.

In its recently released report " [Decoding India's SaaS Startup Ecosystem](https://inc42.com/reports/decoding-indias-saas-startup-ecosystem-report-2024-infocus-saas-in-an-ai-first-world/)", Inc42 highlights that the Indian SaaS Startups sector is poised for growth in the next decade, with over $20 Bn invested in the last 10 years. Currently India has 2.2 K+ Total Funded SaaS Startups from which 62.2% are headquartered in India.

But after seeing the highest uptick from 2021 to 2022, with 348 and 374 deals in those respective years, there has been a decrease in the number of deals per year. This reflects a fundamental shift in how investors are approaching SaaS ventures.

Gone are the days of chasing sky-high valuations purely based on growth projections. Today’s investors are bringing a new level of rationality to the table—focused on sustainable business models, operational efficiency, and the strategic use of AI to drive profitability.

![](<Base64-Image-Removed>)The amount of funding raised and the number of deals by Indian SaaS Startups in the last 10 years.

[Rohit Goyal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rohit-goyal/), Fund Manager at **Windrose Capital**, shared his experience that ‘SaaS startups are no longer receiving the same valuations they did five years ago, despite having similar annual revenues. This marks a notable shift in investors behavior and market expectations.’

He added that this wasn’t the case five years ago. During 2020-21, tech startups were receiving valuations of 10 to 15 times their annual revenue simply for being labeled as a "SaaS Startup." However, today, the ecosystem has become more rational, and a startup with the same annual revenue may only receive a valuation of 5 to 10 times. As a result, companies now need to demonstrate stronger performance to secure higher valuations.

The rise of Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a big reason for these ups and down in the landscape. As per the report 85% Of Indian SaaS Ventures Have Jumped On The AI Integration.

According to another publication, around 90% of SaaS startups have launched at least one AI feature in the past year. As integration of AI is blurring the line between traditional SaaS and AI-enabled software rapidly.

![](<Base64-Image-Removed>)AI-Driven Disruptive Use Cases Transforming India's SaaS Ecosystem.

These are some of the disruptive use cases of Generative AI transforming the Indian SaaS ecosystem. From content creation to data augmentation, much like how Indian enterprises are increasingly democratizing AI in B2B SaaS:

- Content Creation: Generative AI assists SaaS companies in automating tasks like product description generation, marketing copy, and blog posts. This enables businesses to produce content at scale with efficiency and creativity, enhancing brand reach and engagement.

- Personalization: By leveraging AI, startups can improve user engagement and retention, customizing user experiences through chatbots and recommendation engines. This personalized touch builds loyalty and enhances user satisfaction.

- Automation: AI-driven automation is revolutionizing data entry, report generation, and language localization, helping businesses streamline their workflows and reduce human errors in repetitive tasks.

- Product Development: Generative AI speeds up product development cycles by aiding in prototype creation, generating code snippets, and producing detailed mockups, allowing engineers and designers to focus on high-impact innovations.

- Data Augmentation: For machine learning models, data augmentation is crucial. Generative AI aids in training models, improving their accuracy, robustness, and ensuring they deliver reliable outcomes across various applications.


The impact on product development is equally significant. AI-generated prototypes, mockups, and code snippets are expediting development timelines, empowering teams to bring innovations to market faster than ever before. Meanwhile, data augmentation capabilities are improving machine learning models, enhancing their robustness and reliability across applications—a crucial factor for companies competing in a data-driven world.

Rohit also mentioned, ‘He has seen companies being eaten up by AI. However, those that were able to quickly adapt by integrating AI into their business models managed to regain traction, capturing new market share and securing a solid base of prominent clients.’

In this shifting landscape where mega deals are proportionally drying up, SaaS startups can no longer rely solely on the allure of rapid growth to secure investment. Investors are becoming more rational, and startups must now prove their value through concrete performance, operational efficiency, and a clear path to profitability. Market saturation and growing competition further challenge these companies, making differentiation in a crowded field essential.

With a surge of new SaaS players entering the market, gaining traction has become as challenging as in any other sector.

Yet, India’s SaaS industry remains one of the country’s most promising growth areas, with a projected $70 billion market opportunity by 2030. Generative AI alone represents a $17 billion potential within SaaS by the same year, highlighting the immense opportunities available for companies that can innovate and adapt.

Those that leverage technology, focus on sustainable growth, and stand out in the competitive landscape are well-positioned to capture both market share and investor attention in the years to come.
