## Frequently AskedQuestions

What do AI & ML investors care about most?+−

AI investors focus on technical differentiation, data defensibility, and team credibility above all else. They want evidence that your models solve real problems better than alternatives, that your data advantages are sustainable, and that your team has the depth to execute. Commercialization pathway and realistic unit economics are also critical evaluation criteria.

How do you position pre-revenue AI companies?+−

Pre-revenue AI companies need to anchor their narrative around defensible IP, team pedigree, and milestone-based capital deployment. We help articulate what makes your technology unique, what validation exists from pilots or partnerships, and what specific milestones each capital tranche will fund. Investors expect honest separation between what is proven and what remains hypothesis.

Can AI & ML companies advertise a capital raise?+−

Yes, within the rules of the chosen exemption. We work with your team and legal counsel to ensure all marketing accurately represents capabilities without overstating AI performance. All claims about accuracy, speed, or capability improvements are tied to documented benchmarks. We avoid speculative language and focus on demonstrable evidence and realistic roadmaps.

How do you qualify investors so founders don't waste time?+−

We use technical qualification flows and structured education to surface investors who understand AI economics. Casual interest gets filtered through progressively deeper content about your technology, market, and team. By the time prospects reach your calendar, they have engaged with substantive material and are genuinely evaluating the opportunity.

How long does it take to launch?+−

Most AI and ML campaigns take 6–14 weeks to launch, depending on technical complexity and compliance requirements. Raises with significant technical differentiation may require additional time for content development and investor education materials. We work to compress timelines while maintaining quality across all investor-facing assets.

Do you guarantee fundraising results?+−

No. We provide the investor acquisition system, infrastructure, and campaign execution, not a guarantee of capital raise outcomes. Results depend on many factors including technology readiness, market conditions, and team execution. Our focus is building a disciplined system that maximizes your probability of success.
