## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is OpenVC ?

OpenVC is a free startup fundraising platform that helps founders find the right investors and manage their entire raise. Search 20,000+ verified investors, including venture capitalists, angel investors, family offices, accelerators, and more. Build your target list, send your pitch deck, and track your pipeline all in one place.

### Why do founders raise with OpenVC?

Founders raise with OpenVC because it is designed to cut through the noise and get founders in front of the right investors, fast. With built-in tools for CRM, analytics, and warm intros, it helps you stay organized and improve your chances of getting a reply.

### Who is OpenVC for?

OpenVC is for early-stage startup founders who want to raise capital efficiently. Find investors from dozens of industries including SaaS, AI, fintech, biotech, and more. Whether you’re pre-seed, seed, or Series A, OpenVC helps you find and pitch aligned investors without paying intro fees, aimlessly cold-emailing, or scraping databases.

### Is OpenVC free to use?

Yes, OpenVC is completely free to use. You can search investors, submit your pitch deck, track engagement, and manage your raise—all without paying a cent. Premium features are available, but the core platform is free and always will be.


### How do I pitch investors on OpenVC?

To start pitching investors on OpenVC, create a free account and submit your pitch deck directly through our startup funding platform. Investors receive a unique link to view your deck, and you get analytics on who opens it and how long they spend on it. No cold emails, no guesswork. For more info, check out our [complete guide to fundraising on OpenVC](https://www.openvc.app/blog/how-to-raise-funds-with-openvc).

### Can I raise pre-seed or seed funding on OpenVC?

Absolutely, OpenVC is designed for early-stage fundraising. You’ll find thousands of angel investors, pre-seed VCs, accelerators, incubators, and family offices who are actively backing startups across sectors and geographies. Use OpenVC’s filters to narrow your search and find the right investors for your startup.

### What startups have raised capital with OpenVC?

Some examples of startups that successfully secured funding through OpenVC include Mobly (2.5M seed), Paxum ($1.2M seed), and Laennec AI ($400k pre-seed). OpenVC startups have gone on to raise more than $1 billion from top venture capital firms like YC, Sequoia, Google Ventures, and M12.

### Who is behind OpenVC?

OpenVC was created by [Stephane Nasser](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephanenasser/) and [Lucas Roquilly](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucas-roquilly/)—two founders building tools to make startup fundraising more transparent and accessible. We launched OpenVC to help founders find investors, get replies, and raise smarter. The platform is bootstrapped, community-driven, and built with a lot of heart.
