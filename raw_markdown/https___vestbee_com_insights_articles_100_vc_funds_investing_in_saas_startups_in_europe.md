# 100 VC funds investing in SaaS startups in Europe

In recent years, SaaS and B2B solutions have gained significant prominence in the venture capital scene. [According to](https://dealroom.co/guides/saas) Dealroom, in 2023, 47% of venture capital investments were directed towards startups with a SaaS business model, accounting for nearly half of all venture capital funding. This marks a significant increase over the past decade, indicating a sustained and accelerating trend towards SaaS-focused investments.

[Vestbee](https://www.vestbee.com/) has compiled a list of 100 global and Europe-based VC funds that actively support SaaS startups across the European region. This list includes details on their investment focuses, ticket sizes, and the amount of assets under management. To create this selection, we researched the most active investors worldwide. Relying on data from Crunchbase, we focused on those who have made investments since this period last year.

If you notice any investors missing from this list or see any information that needs updating, please [let us know](mailto:lisa.palchynska@vestbee.com).
