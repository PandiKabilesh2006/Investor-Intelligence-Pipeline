## Which VC Firms in SaaS Are Right for Your Stage?

It helps to know who to talk to, whether you're an early-stage company or preparing for an exit. We've put together a brief overview of which VC firms focus on software companies at each stage.

### Pre-seed and Seed VC Firms in SaaS

This initial funding stage is for getting your idea off the ground, often before you have significant revenue, to build a product and find market fit. Some seed stage VC firms that focus on software companies include New York-based **Work-Bench** and **Contour Venture Partners**, while **SaaS Ventures** is another active investor in this space.

### Early Stage VC Firms in SaaS

Early-stage funding, such as Series A or B, is for startups with a proven product and some traction, aimed at scaling the team and accelerating growth. For founders seeking early stage venture capital firms, **Illuminate Ventures** targets B2B software, **Amino Capital** is a well-known investor in AI and SaaS startups, and **Mercury** focuses on SaaS and cloud platforms.

### Late Stage VC Firms in SaaS

Late-stage capital is for established companies looking to expand into new markets, make acquisitions, or prepare for an initial public offering (IPO). Among the major venture capital firms in this category, **Salesforce Ventures** is a prolific corporate VC in enterprise software, and **Fin Capital** specializes in B2B fintech software.

## FAQs about Venture Capital Firms Focused on SaaS

### What is the difference between seed and Series A VC firms?

Seed firms typically back pre-revenue ideas to help find product-market fit. Series A firms invest after you have traction and a proven product, providing capital to scale your team and accelerate growth.

### What do early-stage VC firms look for in a SaaS company?

They look for a strong founding team, a large addressable market, and early signs of product-market fit. Metrics like monthly recurring revenue (MRR) and customer retention become increasingly important as you approach Series A.

### What defines the SaaS VC scene in New York vs. Silicon Valley?

Silicon Valley has a deep history with technology and a high concentration of firms. New York's scene is known for its strong focus on enterprise SaaS and fintech, with close ties to major industries headquartered there.

### Are there many SaaS venture capital firms in Europe?

Yes, Europe has a growing number of venture capital firms focused on SaaS, with major hubs in London, Berlin, and Paris. Many specialize in B2B software and support companies expanding across the continent's diverse markets.
