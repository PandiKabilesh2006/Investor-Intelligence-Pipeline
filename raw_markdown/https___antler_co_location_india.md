## FREQUENTLY **_—ASKED_** QUESTIONS

### Who should apply to the Antler AI Residency?

Teams at the pre-launch, pre-product, or pre-revenue stage.

### How much funding do you provide?

Antler commits up to ~$470K (~INR 4 crores) per startup for 11% equity, we endeavour to invest in 50-75% companies that make it to the Residency

### What does the Antler AI Residency program include?

**Designed for exceptional founding teams:**

1. Chance to get 4Cr in investments upfront, for 11% equity, with an investment decision in just 3 weeks
2. Perks: $1M in AI-Relevant Perks; Up to $4M in Total Perks
3. Benefit from weekly office hours with Antler's GPs as well as a dedicated team, designed to rigorously stress-test venture scalability and validate your idea
4. 2 Weeks of intense programming and 1-1 sessions with seasoned AI builders and operators who reveal their playbook across product, fundraise, GTM and more
5. Get access to an exclusive office space in Bengaluru

### Do I need to relocate to participate?

No, our Residency takes place in-person in Bengaluru only for the first 2 weeks. It provides invaluable in-person access to mentorship, networking, and 1-1 collaborative sessions with other founders and top AI operators and builders. Founders are supported with a one-time INR 2L grant to cover expenses, complimentary office space, and access to over $1Mn worth of founder perks.

### How long does the program last?

Founding teams looking to raise their first institutional round can secure an investment decision within 3 weeks.

### Who can join Before Day Zero?

Aspiring founders, experienced operators, and domain experts thinking seriously about launching a company in the near future.

### What happens inside the Before Day Zero community?

Dedicated support around helping you find a co-founder, curated social and professional events, small group discussions, and access to resources that help you navigate the early steps of your startup journey.

### Is there a cost to join?

No. This is an Antler-backed initiative designed to support founders before they take the plunge.

### Will everyone in the AI Residency receive an investment?

While investment isn’t guaranteed, we endeavour to invest in 50–75% of the teams that join.
