## **The State of B2B Venture Capital**

Funding across enterprise SaaS and AI-driven B2B startups remains strong, with early-stage rounds commanding premium valuations when GTM clarity and customer stickiness are proven. The market is rewarding teams that combine technical excellence with commercial discipline.

What this means for founders:

- **Show ROI fast.** Buyers are more cautious — you need clear payback periods and proof that your product drives measurable impact.

- **Prove efficient GTM.** Pipeline quality, win rates, and retention tell a better story than top-line growth.

- **AI is a wedge, not the whole story.** Automation must tie directly to enterprise workflows and business outcomes.

- **Stack capital + customers.** The best rounds pair investors with design partners and early ARR traction.

### How do I pitch investors on OpenVC?
